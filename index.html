ABOUT US

OFFGRID is a streetwear clothing brand that offers oversized T-shirts, graphic tees, hoodies, sweatshirts, baggy pants, cargo pants, jorts, jackets, and other outerwear. It targets teenagers and young adults aged 13–25 who are interested in modern streetwear. The brand combines minimalist designs with comfortable, relaxed fits, offering affordable to mid-range clothing without compromising on quality and style.
